# Flights, video and sync

[← Guide](README.md)

## Load a flight log

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Load a flight log) and save it as `clips/log.webm`._

- Drop a Betaflight Blackbox log (CSV), a GPX or a JSON file on the page, or press Upload log.

Shown as a tip over: `upload-log`.

## Load the flight video

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Load the flight video) and save it as `clips/video.webm`._

- Load the video of the same flight. It stays on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

Shown as a tip over: `load-video`.

## Sync the video with the log

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Sync the video with the log) and save it as `clips/sync.webm`._

- Auto-sync lines up the video with the log from how the picture moves; flips and rolls are the best markers.

Shown as a tip over: `auto-sync`.

## Replay the flight

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Replay the flight) and save it as `clips/replay.webm`._

- Play: the drone flies its real path through the model, with the video in step.

Shown as a tip over: `play`.
