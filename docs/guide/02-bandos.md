# Bandos and the map

[← Guide](README.md)

## Open the Bandos panel

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Open the Bandos panel) and save it as `clips/open-bandos.webm`._

- Bandos holds your places, and ready-made examples to open as a copy.

Shown as a tip over: `examples`.

## Create a bando

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Create a bando) and save it as `clips/create.webm`._

- New bando opens the map.

Shown as a tip over: `new-bando`.

## Place the area on the map

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Place the area on the map) and save it as `clips/place.webm`._

- Search a place, or paste its coordinates.
- Frame the area: move the map, pull the frame by its edges and corners, turn it with the yellow knob.

Shown as a tip over: `search`.

## Generate the 3D model

_Clip: record it in the app (Guide me → ⏺ Generate the 3D model) and save it as `clips/generate.webm`._

- Generate 3D downloads buildings, roads, trees, water and the real ground, and builds the model.
- The model is drawn over the map: each building can be moved, resized, reshaped and turned.

Shown as a tip over: `generate`.
